Around dusk, mention what time the sun goes down tonight — one short line, the
way someone glancing out of the studio window would say it, not a forecast. Tie
it to the hour if it earns it ("the light goes at twenty past eight"). Say it
once and let it go; if you have already marked the sunset today, skip it.
